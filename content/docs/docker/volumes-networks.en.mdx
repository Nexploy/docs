- Create, inspect and delete Docker volumes and networks.
- Import a volume from a `.tar.gz` archive, from the **Volumes** page. The matching export lives in **Administration > Backups** — see [Backups](/en/features/backups).

<Callout title="Nexploy's own volumes and networks are hidden">
  The `nexploy_db`, `nexploy_traefik_acme` and `nexploy_deployer_workdir` volumes, along with the `nexploy_network` and `nexploy_traefik_network` networks, are what run the instance. They never show up in the lists and no route reaches them even with their id in hand: inspect, delete, import and export all answer `404`. Their names are reserved at creation time too.
</Callout>
