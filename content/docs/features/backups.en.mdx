---
title: Backups
description: Scheduled backups of your volumes to object storage
---

Nexploy lets you schedule **backups** of your Docker volumes to object storage.

- **Storage** — S3-compatible buckets (configurable credentials).
- **Scheduling** — configurable frequency (daily, weekly…).
- **Automation** — via the `backup-volume-bucket-storage` node in a pipeline.
