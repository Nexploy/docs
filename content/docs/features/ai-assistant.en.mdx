Nexploy ships with an **AI assistant** you can query in natural language to drive your infrastructure.

## Supported providers

Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Groq, Mistral, xAI, Perplexity and OpenRouter. Configure your provider and API key in the AI settings.

## Model Context Protocol (MCP)

The assistant uses the **MCP** to expose tools that act on Docker, repositories and deployments. It can therefore inspect the state of your resources and perform actions on your behalf.
