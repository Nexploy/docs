title: Organizations
description: Group your repositories and collaborate with a team on a single instance
---

An **organization** groups repositories together with the members allowed to access them. Every repository belongs to an organization, and that organization determines who can see it and act on it.

<Callout title="Why it matters">
  Without organizations, any user with the right role could act on **every**
  resource in the instance. Organization membership is now checked for each
  action on a repository, a build, a container or a domain.
</Callout>

## Two permission levels

Nexploy combines two role systems that should not be confused:

| Level | Roles | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Instance** | `guest`, `developer`, `admin` | Global administration: users, Traefik, registries, SSL certificates, backups, settings |
| **Organization** | `owner`, `admin`, `member` | Resources of one specific organization: repositories, builds, pipelines, environment variables, domains, containers |

Acting on a resource requires **both**: the instance role allows the type of action, the organization role grants access to that particular resource.

A user with the `admin` instance role can reach every organization, so support operations remain possible.

## Organization roles

- **`member`** — browses repositories, starts builds, manages the organization's containers. Cannot create or delete repositories, nor edit environment variables.
- **`admin`** — manages repositories, pipelines, environment variables, domains and deployments. Invites and removes members.
- **`owner`** — everything an `admin` can do, plus renaming and deleting the organization.

## Inviting a member

Invitations happen **in-app**, with no email involved.

1. Open the organization, then **Members**.
2. Click **Invite member** and enter the email address.
3. Pick the role (`member` or `admin`).

The invited person sees the invitation on their **Account** page and accepts or declines it in one click.

<Callout type="warn" title="The account must already exist">
  Because open sign-up is disabled in Nexploy, you can only invite an address
  that matches an existing account. Create the user first from
  **Administration → Users**.
</Callout>

Invitations expire after 7 days. An organization accepts up to 100 members.

## Switching organization

The switcher in the sidebar, next to the logo, moves between organizations. The active organization determines where newly created repositories are attached.

## Creating an organization

From **Organizations → Create organization**. The `developer` or `admin` instance role is required; a `guest` cannot create one.

The creator automatically becomes its `owner`.

## Safeguards

- The **last `owner`** of an organization cannot be removed, demoted, or leave. Transfer ownership first.
- An organization that **still owns repositories** cannot be deleted. Reassign or delete them beforehand.

## Migrating from an earlier version

On upgrade, every existing user automatically receives a personal organization they own, and their repositories are attached to it. No manual action is required and no repository is lost.
